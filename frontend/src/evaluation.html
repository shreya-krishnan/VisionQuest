Dice, IoU, sensitivity and specificity are computed pixel by pixel against a manual annotation. Add one to unlock evaluation.

  1. Upload a fundus image and its matching vessel annotation.
  2. Run classical processing, U-Net, or both.
  3. Scores are calculated from the real outputs and appear here.
Dice / F1—Overlap between prediction and annotation
IoU—Intersection over union
Sensitivity—Share of vessel pixels detected
Specificity—Share of background correctly rejected
